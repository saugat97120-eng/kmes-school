# Cover Letter for Mercantile Communications (register.com.np)

> **Instructions for the School:**
> 1. Copy the text below and print it on your **official School Letterhead** (or include the school's header, address, and phone number at the top).
> 2. Fill in the date, school registration number, and contact details.
> 3. Have the **Principal** or **School Administrator** sign it and apply the **official school stamp (छाप)**.
> 4. Scan or take a clean, readable photo of the signed & stamped letter (file size must be under 800 KB as per Mercantile limits).
> 5. Upload this along with your **School Registration Certificate / PAN document** on [register.com.np](https://register.com.np/).

---

## Official Cover Letter Template

```text
Date: [Insert Date, e.g., October 17, 2026]

To,
The HostMaster,
Mercantile Communication Pvt. Ltd.
Durbar Marg, Kathmandu, Nepal

Subject: Application for Registration of Domain Name "kmes.com.np"

Respected Sir/Madam,

I am writing to formally request the registration of the domain name "kmes.com.np" on behalf of Kaseni Model Secondary English School (KMES), established in 2059 B.S. and located in Kaseni, Nepal.

Our institution provides quality English-medium education from Nursery to Grade 10. We intend to use the domain "kmes.com.np" to launch and host our official school web portal, publish academic notices, share examination results, and communicate important school events and admissions information with our students, parents, and community.

The domain "kmes.com.np" directly corresponds to the acronym of our school (Kaseni Model Secondary English School).

Below are our technical name server details configured on Cloudflare:

  Primary Name Server:    becky.ns.cloudflare.com
  Secondary Name Server:  earl.ns.cloudflare.com

We have attached a copy of our official School Registration Certificate / Institutional PAN document for verification.

We kindly request you to approve and activate the domain name "kmes.com.np" at your earliest convenience.

Thank you for your assistance and support.


Sincerely,
```

## Documents Required by Mercantile (register.com.np)

When submitting or updating your domain request on [register.com.np](https://register.com.np/), upload the following under **Document Upload**:

1. **Cover Letter (Request Letter)**:
   - The letter above, printed on school letterhead, signed, and stamped.
2. **Registration Document**:
   - Institutional Registration Certificate (from Ministry of Education / Local Municipality / Ward Office) OR Institutional PAN Certificate.
3. **Nameservers Configuration**:
   - Ensure the Primary and Secondary nameservers are set to:
     - Primary: `becky.ns.cloudflare.com`
     - Secondary: `earl.ns.cloudflare.com`
